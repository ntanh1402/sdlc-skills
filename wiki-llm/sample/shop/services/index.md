# Services

* [Accounts Service](SVC-accounts/overview.md) - Customer registration, login, and profile; issues session tokens.
* [Cart Service](SVC-cart/overview.md) - Manages the customer's shopping cart until checkout.
* [Catalog Service](SVC-catalog/overview.md) - Serves product and category reads, and owns product writes.
* [Notifications Service](SVC-notifications/overview.md) - Consumes order events and notifies the customer by email and SMS.
* [Orders Service](SVC-orders/overview.md) - Owns the order lifecycle.
* [Payments Service](SVC-payments/overview.md) - Authorizes and captures card payments via Stripe.
* [Search Indexer](SVC-search-indexer/overview.md) - Projects product changes into the search index.
