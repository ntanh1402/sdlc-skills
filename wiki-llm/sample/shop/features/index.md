# Features

* [Accounts](FEAT-accounts/overview.md) - Let customers register, log in, and manage their profile.
* [Cart](FEAT-cart/overview.md) - Let a customer collect items before checkout.
* [Catalog](FEAT-catalog/overview.md) - Let customers browse, view, and search products.
* [Checkout](FEAT-checkout/overview.md) - Allow customers to complete a purchase end-to-end.
* [Order Notifications](FEAT-order-notifications/overview.md) - Tell a customer their order was placed, without blocking checkout.
* [Payments](FEAT-payments/overview.md) - Take card payment for an order without touching card data.
